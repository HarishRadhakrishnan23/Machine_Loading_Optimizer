3. Safety Stock Handling in Batches

If an urgent order (CDD=2026-08-27) and a safety-stock order (CDD=NULL) have same SIZE~CLASS~DESIGN, should they:

Option A: Batch together (safety stock rides along with urgent order)
Option B: Keep separate (safety stock never batches with urgent orders)

Answer: Option A: Batch together (safety stock rides along with urgent order). BUT FLAG THE SAFETY STOCK ORDER SEPARATELY (like differentiate it with a different colour like yellow or something). Just flag the safety stock and make it different so while working with manual operation., a production engineer will be able to decide whether to use it or not. Simple for less time consuming operations like Cone oversize (Op10) the operator will include it (safety stock) and for complex time requiring operations like stem boring or cone finish the operator will exclude it.
