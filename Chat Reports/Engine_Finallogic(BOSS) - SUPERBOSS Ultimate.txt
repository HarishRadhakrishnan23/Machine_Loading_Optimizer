These are the fundamental changes we are going to implement:

1. In MCH_WIP we have two types of Production Orders.,
	1. ORDER_STATUS is "Active". These are Production orders for which the foundry materials are available in shop floor, assigned to those respective Production Orders for Machining Process to start. These orders should be planned as usual (check the CDD, batch the orders according to batching logic and assign to machines based on the operation and priority.) No external logic constraint is required.
	2. ORDER_STATUS is "Planned". These are Production Orders for which the foundry materials are C.N.A (Currently Not Available) in the shop floor, but materials assigned for those respective production orders but the date at which the foundry materials will arrive on the shop floor will be on their respective PRODUCTION_START_DATE_AND_TIME column of MCH_WIP. In this case the Engine1 has to plan the production to start for that order at PRODUCTION_START_DATE_AND_TIME + 1 day or near (consider that dates (PRODUCTION_START_DATE_AND_TIME + 1) first shift also) and to be delivered before its CDD. 
Always EVERY PRODUCTION ORDER SHOULD BE TRIED TO DELIVER ON OR BEFORE ITS CDD.

2. BATCHING Logic
	I will explain the Batching Logic with more specfic Example down below, but try to understand from this explanation and correlate it with the logic.
		1. Now the Whole concept of adding SETUP_TIME before every First Piece is lost and rather we add a Concept of Fixture and Locator. See, every SIZE~CLASS~DESIGN will have Locator attached to a Fixture. See every PARTICULAR SIZE~CLASS~DESIGN will have Fixed Fixture and Locator and every similar SIZE~CLASS will have Fixture same but only the Locator change.
		2. When the Fixture is changed, both the FIXTURE_CHANGE_TIME (Time taken to change the Fixture) and LOCATOR_CHANGE_TIME (Time taken to change the Locator) from the MCH_ITEMWISE_FIXTURE_LOCATOR table, is added before the first piece of that batch.
		3. When only the Locator is changed, the LOCATOR_CHANGE_TIME from the MCH_ITEMWISE_FIXTURE_LOCATOR table, is added.
		4. Apart from this LOAD_UNLOAD_TIME is added for every piece in that batch so LOAD_UNLOAD_TIME should be multiplied with total quantity of that Batch which Engine1 has grouped.
		5. Same SIZE~CLASS~DESIGN and Same operation should not be ideally carried out in different machine at same time or instance ., For Example if an 10~150~DF order is planned for Op10 today., I want the algorithm to combine all the orders of same 10~150~DF on operation 10 with same Fixture and Locator (or) same Fixture but different Locator to be grouped together on the same day at maximum cases.
		6. A fixed SIZE~CLASS~DESIGN will have fixed FIXTURE and LOCATOR in MCH_ITEMWISE_FIXTURE_LOCATOR., in cases where orders of batch may consist of both Priority orders (CDD is not null) and Safety Stock order (where CDD is null) and when these batches go through higher time consuming Task like VB03 (Weld overlay), Stemboring, Cone Finish and StemBore + Cone Finish (VB04, VB05, VB06), It is usually better to go with batch without Safety stock. In those case u might consider safety stock as last priority., but I still need your help on how to handle this situation. 
		   6.1. But An important thing is in MCH_FIXTURE_LOCATOR table you will give you information on how many fixture (DEVICE_NAME = (Fixture ID) and DEVICE_TYPE = "F") are available on that particular Fixture ID in our shop floor material stock and simililarly No of Locators (DEVICE_NAME = (Locator ID) and DEVICE_TYPE = "L") of that particular Locator ID in our shop floor material stock. We HAVE TO add two new columns in MCH_SCHEDULE_OUTPUT (Engine1 result) in such a way that every time we assign a production order of an operation to a Machine we have to record the Fixture ID and Locator ID from DEVICE_NAME of MCH_FIXTURE_LOCATOR and record it in the two new columns created in MCH_SCHEDULE_OUTPUT, this is done because there are only a fixed no of Fixtures and Locators., and the batching should take that into consideration.

3. Planning Production Continously :
	1. DO NOT PLAN SHIFT WISE., the plan should always be continuous. If a batch with a specific operation is completed in a Machine., that Machine should not be left ideal for not more than 20 mins., that is after completion of a Batch activity and 20 mins of cooling period that machine should be given the next task at 21st minute ( > or = 20 mins ). It doesn't matter if the 21st minute falls on same shift (or) the next shift (or) next available shift on a different day. Production should always be not ideal.
	2. DO NOT have Capacity Restrictions on any Machine. All the Machines can load Infinte Capacity and Time. Production orders of specific Operation is alloted to Machine only based on Batch Priority, Machine Availability, Fixture and Locator availability and Maximum Machine Utilization.
	3. See always consider all the Machine available into consideration while planning., do not plan only on a few machines and keep some machine ideal. If Machine is breakdown or unavailable then that information will be available on MCH_MACHINE_AVAILABILITY_BY_DATE, with which you can plan accordingly, but if there is no data regarding a machine then always consider that machine is available for all AVAILABLE_MINS (in MCH_MACHINE_AVAILABILITY, for all shifts all day and include it in plan.
	

FOR BATCHING EXAMPLE: (Lets take a few orders and try to understand the Batching Logic concept)
Suppose there 7 Orders for which the Order information is given below:
	
| Priority no (Lower the CDD, Higher the Priority) | SIZE | CLASS | DESIGN | QTY | FIXTURE ID | LOCATOR ID |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| 1 | 10 | 150 | DF | 5 | A | 1 |
| 6 | 10 | 150 | LUG | 3 | A | 2 |
| 5 | 10 | 150 | DF | 4 | A | 1 |
| 4 | 10 | 300 | DF | 3 | A | 3 |
| 7 | 14 | 150 | DF | 5 | B | 8 |
| 2 | 14 | 150 | DF | 5 | B | 8 |
| 3 | 16 | 150 | DF | 3 | B | 9 |

Here in this case how would you process or Prioritise the orders., It is simple :
| 1 | 10 | 150 | DF | 5 | A | 1 | is Priority no 1 (Higher Priority (1) and since it is the first piece both FIXTURE_CHANGE_TIME and LOCATOR_CHANGE_TIME should be Added) so it is batched with,
| 5 | 10 | 150 | DF | 4 | A | 1 | so this will be batched with Priority no 1 (Similiar SIZE~CLASS~DESIGN, FIXTURE and LOCATOR) and will be no 2,
| 4 | 10 | 300 | DF | 3 | A | 3 | followed by this order because only locator change (only LOCATOR_CHANGE_TIME is included) is required and it is of higher priority (4) because of which this will be no 3
| 6 | 10 | 150 | LUG | 3 | A | 2 | followed by this because it will be only locator change (only LOCATOR_CHANGE_TIME is included), it will be no 4
| 2 | 14 | 150 | DF | 5 | B | 8 | followed by this order, because Higher Priority (2) (here Both FIXTURE_CHANGE_TIME and LOCATOR_CHANGE_TIME will be added here),so it will be no 5 and which is batched with,
| 7 | 14 | 150 | DF | 5 | B | 8 | same fixture and locator type (so no extra time is added), so it will be at no 6,
| 3 | 16 | 150 | DF | 3 | B | 9 | at last this orders is scheduled at no 7 (same fixture so only LOCATOR_CHANGE_TIME is included)

But For Every batch and QUANTITY we have to add LOAD_UNLOAD_TIME.
