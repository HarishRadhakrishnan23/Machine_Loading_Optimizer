Why it's hard

Four constraints,

Sequence locks

Operations must run in strict order - no step can start before the one before it finishes.

Batch continuity

A batch must stay on one machine, and setup time depends on what ran there just before.

Re-planning is a rebuild

Elevate one urgent order and the whole downstream schedule has to be reworked by hand.

No early warning
